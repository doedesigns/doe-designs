PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bang Bang is a conceptual motion graphic music video created through animation, compositing, and visual storytelling. Inspired by the classic song and Nancy Sinatra’s iconic recording, the project reimagines the track through a vintage western-inspired visual style.

The goal was to translate the rhythm, emotion, and narrative of the song into a complete animated experience. Through storyboard development, illustration, compositing, and timing, the project explores how motion design can transform music into a visual story.

DESIGN APPROACH

The project explores how illustration, compositing, and animation in Adobe After Effects can transform a musical composition into a visual narrative. The visual direction combines retro western imagery, bold graphic elements, and rhythmic animation to create a playful interpretation of the song’s themes of rivalry, confidence, and nostalgia. The project began with storyboard development and visual planning before moving into illustration, compositing, and final animation.
