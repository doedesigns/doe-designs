PROJECT OVERVIEW

My Deets is a conceptual mobile application designed to help users organize and securely access important personal information in one centralized location. The app explores how everyday details, such as financial accounts, personal records, and essential documents, can be structured into a simple and user-friendly digital experience.

The goal was to create an intuitive interface that reduces the stress of managing scattered information while providing users with a clear system for storing, reviewing, and updating their personal data.

CREATIVE DIRECTION

The visual direction combines a clean interface, approachable typography, and color-coded categories to make information easier to navigate and understand. Rounded shapes, organized layouts, and consistent component design create a friendly experience while maintaining the structure needed for managing sensitive personal details.

The project focused on developing a complete mobile design system, including navigation, category organization, and detailed information screens. High-fidelity prototypes were created in Figma to demonstrate how the app functions as a cohesive product from initial entry through everyday use.

PROCESS

The process began by identifying the challenges users face when managing large amounts of personal information across multiple areas of their lives. Research into existing organization and travel applications helped reveal opportunities to improve clarity, navigation, and user confidence while creating a more comprehensive solution.

The app structure was developed around simplifying complex information into intuitive categories, allowing users to quickly locate documents, review important details, and customize their experience. Wireframes established the foundation for navigation and content hierarchy, while the high-fidelity prototype explored visual styling, interaction patterns, and a cohesive interface system designed to feel approachable, secure, and easy to use.
