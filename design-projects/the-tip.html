01

THE PUBLICATION

The Tip is a conceptual volleyball coaching magazine created to celebrate the strategy, leadership, and dedication behind competitive volleyball. Designed for coaches, players, and fans, the publication brings the people and stories behind the sport into focus.

The project centered on creating a cohesive editorial identity, from the magazine cover to a two-page feature spread profiling coach Lisa T.

Magazine cover — establishing the publication's identity.

02

CREATIVE DIRECTION

The visual direction combines deep navy, fresh court green, black, and white with bold athletic typography. Strong headlines and structured layouts give the magazine a competitive, modern feel while keeping the content readable.

The interior spread extends this identity through photography, pull quotes, article hierarchy, and a consistent grid. Together, the cover and feature layout create a publication that feels energetic without sacrificing editorial clarity.

Initial sketches — exploring composition and hierarchy.

03

EDITORIAL DEVELOPMENT

Inspired by my own experience playing volleyball, I wanted to highlight the strategy, dedication, and leadership behind the sport. I collaborated with coach Lisa T., who gave permission to use her photography and share her coaching story.

Using the provided photography, I developed the cover and feature spread through research, sketching, and experimentation with typography, image placement, and page structure. Playful volleyball-themed placeholder text added personality to the article, while photo credits were designed to respect the privacy of the young athletes.

Photography selection — coach and team imagery.

04

THE FEATURE SPREAD

The two-page spread brings the feature to life through a structured editorial grid, supporting photography, pull quotes, and clear reading flow. The result extends the magazine's visual identity beyond the cover and into a complete reading experience.

Two-page editorial spread — layout, typography, and storytelling.
