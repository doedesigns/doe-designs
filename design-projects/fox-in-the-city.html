PROJECT OVERVIEW

Fox in the City is a conceptual book cover design project created for fictional author Charlie Leefang. The story follows Vivica, a clever and resourceful fox who leaves her familiar forest home behind to begin a new life in Seattle. As she navigates the unfamiliar world of city living, Vivica discovers that adapting to a new environment requires creativity, confidence, and a willingness to embrace change.

The goal was to create a cover design that captures the contrast between the natural world and urban life while introducing Vivica's curious and charismatic personality. Through illustration, typography, imagery, and composition, the project explores how editorial design can establish a story's setting, tone, and character before the reader opens the book.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction blends organic textures with modern city elements to reflect Vivica's transition from forest to urban surroundings. Cover concepts explored different approaches to character, environment, and storytelling before developing a final design system that combines warmth, curiosity, and a sense of adventure across the front cover, spine, and back cover.
