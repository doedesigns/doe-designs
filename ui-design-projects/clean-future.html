A CONCEPTUAL NONPROFIT BRAND & WEBSITE

Project Overview

Turning environmental awareness into meaningful action.

Clean Future Network is a conceptual environmental nonprofit designed to make nature conservation and community cleanup more approachable. The website brings together environmental education, sustainability resources, and opportunities for people to connect and take action.

DESIGNED FOR PEOPLE AT EVERY STAGE

Who Is It For?

  • The curious beginner: People who want to help but aren't sure where to start.
  • The new community member: People looking for local initiatives and like-minded individuals.
  • The experienced advocate: People ready to share environmental knowledge and opportunities.

PURPOSE MEETS PRACTICALITY

Making Environmental Action Accessible

01 / THE CHALLENGE

Awareness doesn't always lead to action.

People may want to help protect the environment but lack the information, resources, or community connections to know where to begin.

02 / THE OPPORTUNITY

Make getting involved easier.

Create a welcoming resource that organizes environmental information, highlights opportunities, and connects people with their communities.

RESEARCH, INSPIRATION & DISCOVERY

From Research to Design

I reviewed environmental and nature-focused nonprofit websites to explore how they organize information, present educational content, and encourage community involvement. I also looked beyond the environmental space for interaction ideas, including a dynamic visitor-comment feature that inspired the homepage community panel.

01

Clarity

Make educational resources and community information easy to find.

02

Connection

Use a natural visual language and welcoming community-focused content.

03

Participation

Give visitors reasons to explore, contribute, and get involved.

DESIGNING FOR DISCOVERY

01 / Rotating Page Headlines

Each page pairs a unique introductory phrase with a changing word—connecting environmental responsibility with people, nature, health, safety, and the future. The movement adds personality while inviting visitors to pause and explore.

DESIGNING FOR CONNECTION

02 / Community Comments

A little personality. A lot of community.

Visitors can submit comments through the Contact page and choose to have their contributions appear on the homepage. Comments cycle through the vertical panel, creating a changing snapshot of community participation.

The warm peach panel adds visual contrast while making the experience feel more personal and welcoming.

STRUCTURE · IDENTITY · APPLICATION

From First Sketch to Finished Experience

01 / STRUCTURE

Plan the Experience

Sketches established the page hierarchy, navigation, and content structure before moving into Figma.

02 / IDENTITY

Define the Visual Language

A nature-inspired palette, typography, and imagery created a calm, approachable visual identity.

03 / APPLICATION

Bring It to Life

The designs were refined in Figma and developed into a multi-page website using Dreamweaver.

Licensed imagery was sourced through Envato Elements to support the themes and purpose of each page.

INDEPENDENT DEVELOPMENT

Beyond the Classroom

I wanted to create a polished, usable experience rather than a typical classroom assignment.

I built the website in Dreamweaver and independently explored additional techniques to bring the concept to life, including custom interactions and a layered footer. Because JavaScript wasn't covered in the course, I learned the fundamentals needed to create the rotating headline behavior.

REFLECTION & NEXT STEPS

Review & Next Steps

I personally checked the navigation and links throughout the coded website. Formal usability testing with representative visitors was outside the scope of the assignment.

Given additional time, I would test how easily first-time visitors find environmental resources, understand the rotating headlines, and discover ways to participate. I would also evaluate whether the community comment feature encourages engagement.
