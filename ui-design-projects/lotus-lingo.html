A CONCEPTUAL LANGUAGE-LEARNING APPコンセプトの語学学習アプリ

PROJECT OVERVIEWプロジェクト概要

Lotus Lingo is a conceptual Japanese-learning app shaped by my own experience teaching myself the language with several different learning tools. I wanted to bring the most useful parts of that experience into one place while addressing a few recurring frustrations: unclear lesson progression, repetitive practice, and limited ways to use a language beyond the lesson itself.

The concept connects guided lessons with writing and speaking practice, games, music discovery, and community spaces. Its structure is designed to help learners understand what to do next while still giving them room to choose how they practice. I developed more than 100 high-fidelity screens to explore the product across onboarding, learning, motivation, and social features.

THE DESIGN OPPORTUNITYデザインの課題

CHALLENGE & SOLUTION課題と解決策

01

THE CHALLENGE

Help learners build momentum without making the experience feel repetitive.

Learning Japanese involves more than memorizing vocabulary. Learners move between different writing systems, written and spoken practice, and formal and informal language. They also need a clear sense of progression and reasons to return between lessons.

02

THE SOLUTION

Connect structured learning with meaningful ways to practice.

Lotus Lingo pairs a guided lesson sequence with distinct writing and speaking activities, games, rewards, music discovery, and level-based community rooms. The goal is to make the next step easy to find while offering different ways to reinforce what learners are studying.

A CLEAR PATH INTO LEARNING学習への明確な道筋

ONBOARDING FLOWオンボーディングの流れ

Onboarding introduces the app's learning approach, helps new learners get oriented, and creates a clear route from first visit to first lesson. Each step is intended to make the next action feel understandable rather than leaving learners to navigate a collection of disconnected features.

  1. 01

    Welcome

    Set expectations and introduce the app's approach to learning Japanese.

  2. 02

    Initial Setup

    Establish the learner's starting point and move through the initial setup.

  3. 03

    Learning Introduction

    Show how lessons and practice activities fit into the learning experience.

  4. 04

    First Lesson

    Move from orientation into a structured first lesson.

  5. 05

    Practice

    Reinforce the lesson with an interactive practice activity.

  6. 06

    Continue Learning

    Give learners a clear route into the app's ongoing lesson and practice options.
