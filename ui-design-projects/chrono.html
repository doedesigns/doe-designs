PROJECT OVERVIEW

A fictional technology company.
An impossible product.
A credible digital experience.

Phasepoint Technologies introduces the Chrono Nexus, a fictional device designed for controlled time travel.
The goal was to make this extraordinary concept feel like a sophisticated, credible, thoughtfully designed technology product.

THE CHALLENGE

How do you make an impossible technology feel understandable & trustworthy?

  • Unify product information, training, destinations, & policies.
  • Make unfamiliar risks & requirements easy to understand.
  • Build trust through a clear, coherent, & professional digital experience.

THE SOLUTION

Treat time travel like a legitimate technology product, not a science-fiction fantasy.

  • Build a cohesive brand & connected digital product ecosystem.
  • Guide users from discovery & preparation through travel & account management.
  • Communicate complex systems through clear information architecture, safety protocols, & realistic product content.

THE CONCEPT IN FOCUS

PROJECT APPROACH

RESEARCH & DISCOVERY

Defined the product concept, its constraints, & its users.

  • Established technology rules & safety requirements.
  • Identified traveler needs & essential content.
  • Organized the information architecture & content priorities.

PROCESS

Translated the concept into a connected digital experience.

  • Developed the brand & visual identity.
  • Planned user journeys & page structures.
  • Designed interfaces & interactions.
  • Refined layouts & flows in Figma.

DESIGN OUTCOME

A cohesive digital ecosystem built around a fictional product.

  • Brand identity & product presentation.
  • Training, destination, & traveler experiences.
  • Device, account, & dashboard interfaces.
  • Interactive high-fidelity prototype.

CREATIVE DIRECTION & PROCESS

Making the impossible feel credible.

A dark interface, controlled white & blue accents, geometric forms, & clean typography establish a visual language inspired by aerospace technology & scientific instrumentation.

A recurring circular motif suggests temporal movement, while the structured layouts reinforce precision & clarity. Dry humor appears in the product copy & corporate messaging, adding personality without compromising the professional presentation.
