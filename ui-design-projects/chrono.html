PROJECT OVERVIEW

A fictional technology company. An impossible product.
A credible digital experience.

Phasepoint Technologies introduces the Chrono Nexus, a fictional device for controlled time travel. The challenge was to make this extraordinary technology feel like a sophisticated, believable product.

THE CHALLENGE

How do you help people understand, prepare for, and safely navigate a technology that doesn't exist?

  • Unify product information, training, destinations, and policies.
  • Make unfamiliar risks and requirements easy to understand.
  • Build trust through a coherent, professional experience.

THE SOLUTION

Treat time travel like a legitimate technology product, not a science-fiction fantasy.

  • Build a cohesive brand and digital product ecosystem.
  • Connect discovery, preparation, travel, and account management.
  • Use clear systems, safety protocols, and realistic product content.

CREATIVE DIRECTION & PROCESS

Presenting an impossible technology with the visual language of a credible technology company.

A dark interface, controlled cyan and blue accents, geometric forms, and clean typography create a visual language inspired by aerospace and scientific instrumentation.

The circular graphic system references temporal movement. Dry humor lives in the product copy and corporate messaging, while the interface remains professional and restrained.
