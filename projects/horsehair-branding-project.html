PROJECT OVERVIEW

HorseHair Shower Gel is a conceptual luxury shower product inspired by equestrian style, natural ingredients, and premium self-care. The goal was to create a brand identity that felt elegant, memorable, and approachable.

The identity combines refined typography, bold imagery, and a clean product system designed to translate across packaging, advertising, and digital applications.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction blends premium equestrian aesthetics with modern personal care branding. Typography, photography, packaging, and advertising applications were designed as a unified luxury brand system.

Mascott combination logo showing bright lively horse.

Social media advertising concept designed to extend the HorseHair identity into digital spaces.

Product application showcasing the HorseHair brand system across packaging and product presentation.

Large-format billboard application demonstrating brand visibility and messaging.

Brand guidelines documenting the visual system, typography, color palette, and standards created for HorseHair Shower Gel.
