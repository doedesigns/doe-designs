PROJECT OVERVIEW

HorseHair Shower Gel is a conceptual haircare brand developed for consumers who challenge traditional beauty standards and embrace individuality. The brand combines fantasy-inspired characters, bold color contrasts, and unconventional visual storytelling to create a distinctive identity designed to stand apart from traditional personal care products.

The visual system blends dark atmospheric backgrounds with vibrant orange and blue accents, creating a rebellious yet imaginative brand experience centered around self-expression and embracing what makes each person unique.

DESIGN APPROACH

The HorseHair identity was designed around the idea of beauty beyond convention. Fantasy-inspired characters, expressive illustrations, and unexpected visual elements were developed to represent a community of consumers who are confident, creative, and willing to go against the grain.

The branding system incorporates bold typography, dramatic imagery, and a memorable illustrated horse mascot to create a recognizable personality across advertising, packaging, and digital applications.

An illustrated horse mascot created as a central visual element of the HorseHair brand identity. Featuring a vibrant orange palette and flowing mane and tail, the character reinforces the brand’s connection to hair, movement, and personality while providing a memorable symbol across marketing applications.

A social media campaign concept extending the HorseHair identity into digital spaces through character-driven storytelling. The design features a fantasy-inspired character with bold blue and blonde hair, emphasizing creativity, individuality, and the brand’s celebration of unconventional personal style.

A large-scale advertising concept designed to create immediate brand recognition in a public environment. The billboard combines fantasy-inspired character design, expressive hair styling, and the HorseHair color palette to create a visually striking campaign focused on confidence and standing apart from the crowd.

A product packaging application showcasing how the HorseHair identity translates into a physical consumer product. The dark packaging, vibrant color accents, and illustrated horse mascot create a premium yet unconventional appearance designed to appeal to consumers seeking a haircare brand with personality.

A client-facing brand guide created to establish the visual foundation of the HorseHair identity. The guide presents logo usage, typography, color direction, imagery, and brand recommendations to ensure consistency across advertising, packaging, and future applications.
