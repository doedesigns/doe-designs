PROJECT OVERVIEW

Driftwood Outdoor Gear is a conceptual outdoor lifestyle brand created to celebrate exploration, durability, and connection with the natural environment. The goal was to develop a brand identity that feels authentic to outdoor enthusiasts while communicating reliability, adventure, and everyday performance.

The identity combines rugged visual elements, refined typography, and a cohesive product system designed to translate across outdoor equipment, apparel, packaging, and brand communications.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction balances the adventurous spirit of outdoor exploration with a modern, approachable aesthetic. Branding elements were developed across product applications, apparel, and marketing materials to create a unified identity built around adventure, quality, and environmental connection.
