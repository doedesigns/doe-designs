PROJECT OVERVIEW

Driftwood Outdoor Gear is a conceptual sustainable outdoor brand created around the idea that exploration and environmental responsibility can exist together. The brand focuses on producing outdoor equipment made from recycled materials, encouraging consumers to protect the environments they enjoy exploring.

The identity system was designed to communicate sustainability, adventure, and connection with nature through a recognizable visual language that extends across product design, packaging, and brand applications.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction combines natural elements, modern product design, and environmental storytelling to create a brand that feels both adventurous and responsible. The logo incorporates three leaves arranged to reference the universal recycling symbol, creating an immediate connection between the brand mission and its visual identity.

Relief-map patterns, earthy color influences, and clean outdoor-inspired layouts were developed to create a cohesive system that could translate across gear, apparel, packaging, and future brand experiences.

Brand guidelines documenting the foundational elements of the Driftwood Outdoor Gear identity, including logo usage, typography, color direction, and visual standards. The soft relief-map background reinforces the brand’s connection to navigation, exploration, and the natural landscape.

Extended brand guidelines demonstrating how the Driftwood visual system can be applied across product designs, marketing materials, and future outdoor lifestyle applications.

Product mockup demonstrating the Driftwood identity applied to outdoor footwear. The custom relief-map pattern connects the product design to exploration, navigation, and the environments that inspire the brand.

Early concept sketches exploring multiple visual directions for the logo design. Focusing on the recycling element of the brand, these pencil studies tested composition, typography placement, symbolic elements, and overall mood while refining the visual approach.

An editorial magazine spread designed for Driftwood Outdoor Gear, combining rugged outdoor imagery with informative storytelling and bold visual design. The layout highlights scenic destinations, outdoor experiences, and the Driftwood brand while balancing photography, illustration, typography, and structured editorial content. Presented in a natural outdoor setting alongside recycled essential gear, the mockup reinforces the brand’s connection to exploration, adventure, and life beyond the trail.

Outdoor accessory application showcasing how the Driftwood brand system extends beyond primary gear into everyday adventure products. The patterned design reinforces consistency while creating a recognizable lifestyle identity.

This outdoor advertisement for Driftwood Outdoor Gear uses a bold visual contrast to highlight the environmental impact of discarded footwear. Set against a rugged mountain landscape, the design follows two hikers alongside a growing pile of plastic waste, reinforcing the message that worn-out boots can have a second life through recycling. Combining impactful imagery, sustainable messaging, and clear brand identity, the advertisement encourages consumers to think beyond the trail and consider where their gear goes next.
