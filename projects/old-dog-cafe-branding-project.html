PROJECT OVERVIEW

Old Dog Cafe is a conceptual community cafe created to support senior dogs in need of adoption. The space combines a welcoming coffee shop, dog play yard, and attached ice cream parlor where guests can connect with adoptable dogs while enjoying a unique cafe experience.

The goal was to create a brand identity that celebrated the personality, comfort, and companionship of older dogs while communicating the cafe's mission of providing ongoing care for rescue dogs and continued support for adopted pets.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction blends warmth, nostalgia, and playful character to create a brand that feels approachable and community-focused. Branding elements were developed across signage, menus, packaging, environmental graphics, promotional materials, and digital applications to create a cohesive experience centered around connection, compassion, and animal advocacy.
