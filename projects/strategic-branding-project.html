PROJECT OVERVIEW

Strategic Estate Planning is a boutique estate planning law firm brand created to transform a complex legal process into a clear and approachable client experience. Inspired by the idea of estate planning as strategic puzzle solving, the brand reflects the careful organization, planning, and personalized guidance required to protect a client’s goals during life and after death.

The project included the development of a complete visual identity system across client communications, marketing materials, print applications, and a customized website experience designed to communicate trust, expertise, and thoughtful planning.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction balances professionalism and approachability through refined typography, structured layouts, and a modern editorial style. The identity was designed to create confidence while making complex estate planning concepts easier for clients to understand.

Each application was developed as part of a cohesive client journey — from initial information gathering and engagement documents to educational resources, marketing materials, and digital experiences. The final system creates a consistent and trustworthy brand presence across every client interaction.

Conference brochure created as a client-facing marketing tool to introduce Strategic Estate Planning services in a clear and approachable format. The design balances professional credibility with accessible messaging to help prospective clients better understand the value of proactive estate planning.

The Strategic Estate Planning moodboard explores the visual direction for the brand through curated advertisements, fictional character references, typography, color palettes, logo inspiration, and website interface elements. These references helped define a professional yet approachable identity designed to build trust while making estate planning feel more accessible and engaging.

Initial logo explorations for Strategic Estate Planning, documenting the development process from rough sketches to refined brand concepts. These studies examined different visual directions, iconography, and typographic treatments to identify a solution that balanced professionalism with approachability.

Branded engagement letter template designed to create a confident and professional first impression while guiding clients through the beginning stages of their estate planning relationship.

Branded invoice system created to extend the Strategic Estate Planning identity into everyday client interactions while maintaining a consistent, polished, and professional experience.

Print advertising concept developed for submission to the WSBA Bar Association Magazine. The advertisement communicates Strategic Estate Planning’s expertise through a refined editorial style designed to appeal to clients seeking knowledgeable and personalized estate planning guidance.

Client questionnaire designed to transform the initial information-gathering process into a structured and approachable experience. The layout organizes complex personal and financial information while reinforcing the firm’s thoughtful planning approach.

Educational planning resource created to help high-net-worth clients visualize how their assets could be managed throughout life and distributed after death. The design presents complex financial planning concepts in a clear, organized format to support client understanding and decision-making.

Website experience designed and customized in Wix to introduce Strategic Estate Planning services while guiding visitors through complex legal concepts with clarity and confidence. The website combines strategic content organization, approachable messaging, and intuitive navigation to create a professional digital experience for prospective clients.
