PROJECT OVERVIEW

MontraPac is a conceptual brand identity project focused on creating a modern outdoor backpack with a strong visual presence across multiple advertising platforms. The goal was to develop a memorable brand that could translate effectively from product packaging into promotional and lifestyle applications.

The identity combines bold graphic elements, consistent typography, and a cohesive visual system designed to create recognition across digital advertising, apparel, and large-scale promotional materials.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction emphasizes brand consistency and versatility across consumer touchpoints. Packaging, advertising, apparel, and environmental applications were designed as part of a unified identity system that balances functionality, visibility, and contemporary brand appeal.
