PROJECT OVERVIEW

Barking Dog Bookstore is a conceptual independent bookstore brand designed to create a welcoming community centered around reading, discovery, and connection. The goal was to develop an identity that felt approachable, memorable, and reflective of the personality found within a neighborhood bookstore.

The brand system combines expressive typography, playful visual elements, and cohesive print and digital applications designed to engage customers across multiple touchpoints.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction balances the warmth of a local bookstore with a modern entertainment-focused identity. Branding elements were developed across logo design, promotional materials, social media, print collateral, and digital experiences to create a consistent and recognizable brand presence.
