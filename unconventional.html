PROJECT OVERVIEW

Unconventional Life is a conceptual motion design project created as a class exploration of audio, AI-generated imagery, and visual storytelling. The project challenged me to experiment with unconventional combinations of imagery, movement, sound, and atmosphere to create a short-form audiovisual experience.

DESIGN APPROACH

Rather than following a traditional narrative structure, the piece uses unexpected visual transitions and contrasting scenes to explore how AI-generated imagery can be combined with motion and sound to create a cohesive creative experience. The project was an opportunity to experiment with AI as a design tool while exploring how audio can influence pacing, mood, and the overall direction of a motion piece.
