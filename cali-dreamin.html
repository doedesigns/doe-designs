PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bang Bang is a conceptual music video created using Adobe After Effects and Photoshop. Originally performed by Cher, the project reimagines the song through a visual style inspired by Nancy Sinatra's aesthetic and retro cinematic influences.

The goal was to translate the energy, rhythm, and emotion of the music into a cohesive motion experience through visual storytelling, timing, typography, and animated composition.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction combines vintage-inspired imagery, bold graphic elements, and rhythmic animation to create a modern interpretation of a classic song. Storyboarding, compositing, and motion development were used to build a visual narrative that supports the mood and personality of the music.
