Hello!
I'm Jen Doehne
of Doe Designs

GRAPHIC DESIGNER

I'm a multidisciplinary designer with a background spanning law, business, and design. After more than two decades in the legal industry, I returned to my art-kid roots and found a way to bring my passion for creativity together with the skills I've developed throughout my career: strategic thinking, clear communication, organization, attention to detail, and the ability to manage complex projects from concept through completion.

I bring curiosity, determination, and a big-picture perspective to every project. My professional background has taught me to look at problems from multiple angles, identify what matters most, communicate ideas clearly, and balance competing priorities without losing sight of the details. I believe those skills make me not only a stronger designer, but a more thoughtful collaborator and problem-solver.

My design experience spans branding and rebranding, logo development, print and editorial design, digital applications, website design, UX/UI, athletic apparel, and motion graphics. I enjoy moving between disciplines and finding the visual and strategic approach that best serves the project—whether that's developing a brand identity, designing an intuitive digital experience, creating compelling motion, or translating an idea into a clear and engaging visual system.

Ultimately, I want to create work that is visually compelling, purposeful, intuitive, and effective. I bring the perspective of someone who understands both the creative process and the professional demands behind it, along with a genuine curiosity to keep learning, creating, and growing as a designer.
