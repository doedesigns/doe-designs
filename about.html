UI/UX & VISUAL DESIGNER

I'm a UI/UX and visual designer who enjoys turning complex ideas into clear, engaging experiences. With a foundation in user-centered design, brand identity, and visual communication , I bring both creative thinking and a practical, detail-oriented approach to every project.

After more than two decades in the legal industry, I returned to my art-kid roots and found a way to bring my passion for creativity together with the skills I've developed throughout my career: strategic thinking, clear communication, organization, attention to detail, and the ability to manage complex projects from concept through completion.

Before pursuing design, I spent more than a decade working with clients and translating complicated information into thoughtful, accessible solutions. That experience continues to shape how I approach design: understanding the problem, considering the people on the other side of it, and creating work that is both purposeful and visually compelling.
