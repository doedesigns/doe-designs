PROJECT OVERVIEW

Bang Bang is a conceptual motion graphic music video created through illustration, animation, compositing, and visual storytelling. Inspired by Nancy Sinatra's iconic recording of the classic song, the project reimagines the track through a vintage western-inspired visual language that combines bold graphic shapes, stylized characters, and cinematic scene transitions.

The goal was to translate the rhythm, tension, and emotional progression of the song into a cohesive visual narrative rather than simply illustrating the lyrics. Each scene was designed to support the music through movement, pacing, visual contrast, and carefully timed transitions, creating a sense of anticipation and building toward the song's dramatic moments.

DESIGN APPROACH

The visual direction draws from retro western film imagery while incorporating a playful, graphic interpretation of the song's themes of rivalry, confidence, and nostalgia. Character silhouettes, western-inspired environments, typography, and illustrated assets were developed to create a distinctive visual world that could shift between quieter moments and more energetic sequences.

The project began with storyboard development and visual planning to establish the narrative flow, composition, and timing of each scene. Original illustrations and supporting graphic elements were then developed and brought together through compositing and animation in Adobe After Effects. Movement, transitions, and pacing were refined in relation to the music to create a finished piece that demonstrates how motion design can turn a familiar song into an original visual experience.
