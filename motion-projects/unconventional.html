01 / THE CONCEPT

An Ordinary Walk.
An Unordinary World.

What begins as an ordinary walk with two dogs becomes a journey into a world of unexpected characters, surreal encounters, and imaginative possibilities.

Unconventional Life is a fictional sitcom trailer exploring how AI-generated imagery, motion, editing, and sound can transform an everyday premise into something extraordinary.

02 / CREATIVE DIRECTION

Following the Imagination

Familiar settings meet unexpected characters, shifting moods, and moments that blur the line between the ordinary and surreal.

AI-generated imagery established the visual world, while editing, compositing, transitions, and sound brought the individual moments together into a playful sequence with rhythm and personality.

03 / PRODUCTION PROCESS

Experiment. Assemble. Refine.

Building the trailer meant turning a collection of generated visuals and ideas into one cohesive viewing experience.

Adobe Firefly helped develop visual assets, which were then refined in After Effects and Premiere Pro. Compositing, animation, transitions, pacing, and sound design shaped the final trailer.
