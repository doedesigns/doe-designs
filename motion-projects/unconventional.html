01 / THE CONCEPT

An Ordinary Walk. An Unordinary World.

Unconventional Life is a fictional sitcom trailer following a woman and her two dogs through an ordinary world that gradually becomes surreal, unpredictable, and fantastical.

The project explores how AI-generated imagery, editing, motion, and sound can transform an imaginative idea into a cohesive trailer.

02 / CREATIVE DIRECTION

Following the Imagination

AI-generated imagery sets the stage for a shifting visual world, contrasting familiar places with unexpected characters and events.

Editing, compositing, transitions, and sound shape these varied moments into a playful sequence with rhythm and personality.

03 / PRODUCTION PROCESS

Experiment, Assemble, Refine

I experimented with Adobe Firefly to develop visual assets, then refined them using After Effects and Premiere Pro.

Compositing, animation, transitions, pacing, and sound design helped turn the generated material into a finished trailer.
