01 / THE CONCEPT

An Ordinary Walk. An Unordinary World.

Unconventional Life is a fictional TV-show trailer following an imaginative woman and her two dogs through a world where the unexpected is always around the corner. Everyday surroundings give way to surreal encounters, fantastical creatures, and increasingly unpredictable scenes.

The project was an opportunity to experiment with AI-generated video and imagery while exploring how editing, motion, sound, and pacing can bring an imaginative concept together into a cohesive trailer.

02 / CREATIVE DIRECTION

Following the Imagination

AI-generated imagery provided a starting point for a constantly shifting visual experience. Rather than following a conventional storyline, the trailer embraces surprising contrasts between familiar settings and fantastical events.

The creative challenge was to turn those varied images and moments into a watchable sequence, using visual rhythm, transitions, compositing, and sound to give the trailer momentum and personality.

03 / PRODUCTION PROCESS

Experiment, Assemble, Refine

This project focused on learning through experimentation with Adobe Firefly, After Effects, and Premiere Pro. AI-assisted visuals were developed and selected, then brought together through compositing, animation, editing, transitions, and sound design.

The process explored how AI-generated material could be shaped into a finished motion piece through hands-on creative decisions—from choosing and arranging visuals to refining timing, pacing, and the overall viewing experience.
