01 / The concept

Leave the ordinary behind.

MontraPac is a conceptual outdoor lifestyle brand built around the idea that adventure begins when routine ends. The brand draws on the freedom of the outdoors and the appeal of stepping beyond the everyday.

I developed a visual identity around a distinctive mountain symbol, a custom wordmark, and a restrained palette with a bold red accent. The system was designed to carry across product applications, apparel, and advertising campaigns.

02 / Visual identity

Built for the journey.

The identity combines a mountain-inspired symbol, custom letterforms, and a focused color palette. Each element was developed to work independently or together, creating a recognizable system across a range of brand applications.

→

A flexible identity system.

The core logo was developed in alternate treatments to maintain recognition across different backgrounds and campaign contexts. A separate wordmark provides additional flexibility when the full symbol is not needed.

A mark with a hidden detail.

The mountain silhouette conceals the initials M and P within its structure. A red accent draws attention to the P, creating a small visual discovery within the mark. The supporting palette extends the identity into product and campaign applications.

03 / Motion campaign

Choose a different path.

The advertisement moves from the repetitive rhythm of the workplace to the openness of the outdoors. Editing, pacing, and sound help build that contrast before the film resolves into the MontraPac identity and its “Follow Me” campaign message.

Inspired by the choice between comfort and awareness, the film moves from fragmented workplace scenes to slower, immersive outdoor moments before closing on the MontraPac identity. This was a student project - I have no rights in the Matrix soundtrack.

04 / Campaign applications

Adventure, everywhere.

The campaign explores how the identity translates across digital advertising, transit placements, and seasonal promotions. Consistent typography, product imagery, and brand elements connect each execution while allowing the creative direction to respond to its setting.
