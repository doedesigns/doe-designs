A BRAND WITH HEART

Good Food. Good Company. Second Chances.

Old Dog Café is a neighborhood café and specialty ice cream concept built around a simple idea: bring people together over good food while helping senior dogs find loving homes.

The identity balances warmth, wit, and a little mischief, creating a memorable brand across hospitality, merchandise, marketing, and the proposed physical space.

STRATEGIC HIGHLIGHTS

A Café with a Purpose.

  • A distinctive hospitality concept. A neighborhood café, ice cream parlor, and senior-dog adoption mission under one roof.
  • A business built around the guest experience. Creative food and drinks give customers a reason to visit and return.
  • A thoughtful approach to dog welfare. Dedicated retreat areas, outdoor access, and responsible adoption practices help prioritize the dogs' needs.
  • A cohesive brand ecosystem. Menus, apparel, promotional materials, and environmental design work together as one experience.
  • A concept with community potential. Transparent support options create ways for guests to contribute without pressure to donate or adopt.

This is a conceptual business and spatial proposal, not an operating business plan or approved architectural design.
