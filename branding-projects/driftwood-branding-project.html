01 / THE IDEA

Adventure
leaves a mark.

What if the gear we take into the wild helped protect it, too?

Driftwood Outdoor Gear is a conceptual outdoor brand built around recycled materials, responsible exploration, and a second life for everyday essentials.

02 / BRAND IDENTITY

Rooted in
the outdoors.

Three leaves form a circular mark inspired by the familiar recycling symbol. Paired with the Driftwood name, the logo connects circular use of materials with the natural world.

Forest greens and earthy browns ground the identity, while topographic map patterns bring the spirit of the trail into the brand's typography and applications.

From sketch to a trail-inspired identity.
